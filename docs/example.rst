Worked example
==============

The worked notebook demonstrates:

* loading and validating a bounded content measure;
* changing an individual prior with two direct assignments;
* posterior fitting and diagnostics;
* latent-mean and posterior-predictive HDIs;
* comparison with constant, linear, and quadratic models using LOO.
